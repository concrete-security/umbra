# Roadmap

Umbra is working toward its first public beta. Priorities are ordered by security and usability rather than promised dates.

## Public beta gate

- Complete independent licensing and redistribution review.
- Make every released binary and container reproducible from locked public inputs, with notices, SBOMs, provenance, and signature verification.
- Provide an anonymous quick start and a self-host path that clearly names the required third-party services and costs.
- Protect release and deployment workflows against untrusted pull requests.
- Pass the local test suite and the maintainer-operated live journey without attestation or policy bypasses.

## After beta

- Broaden provider portability while preserving attestation and fail-closed egress guarantees.
- Improve Security CVM availability, CA lifecycle, and policy rollout safety.
- Expand policy authoring, observability, and audit ergonomics.
- Define stable compatibility and support windows before 1.0.
- Evaluate post-v0 connector isolation separately from the v0 verifier gate.

The roadmap is directional. Accepted issues and ADRs define committed scope; external service availability and security findings may change ordering.
